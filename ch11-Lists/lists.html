What is HTML Lists?

HTML List is a way to organize information by displaying it one by one. Basically to list down the information one by one. We use HTML List when we want to orginazise any information and list it down into a sequence. Let's Understand with a example below -


Scenario-
David's 22nd birthday is in next 3 days. His current pc is very old (11 years) and he was to upgrade the components of his pc. So, he has to make a list of compnomets he needs to upgrade. So, he visited his nearest shop "Your Dream Build" to get a quotation and the list. There the respresentative named "Rasid" helps him to generate the list. Here's the list whaich Rasid gave to David-


    List of PC Components-
  1. A New 16 GB RAM
  2. Ryzen 7 cpu
  3. 2tb ssd
  4. New Power supply
  5. New CPU case

Now, we understood how HTML Lists are used. Let's see the Syntax of HTML List and know their types (ex- ordered, unordered, description) below -

   <li>Item1</li>
