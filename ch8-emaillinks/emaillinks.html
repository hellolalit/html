Chapter 8 - Email Links in HTML

Understanding Email Links

Email links, also known as mailto links, allow users to quickly open their default email client with a pre-configured email. These links can include not just the recipient's email address, but also the subject line, CC recipients, BCC recipients, and even the email body.

Basic Email Link Syntax

<a href="mailto:example@email.com">Send email</a>

Adding Subject Line

You can pre-fill the subject line using the subject parameter:

<a href="mailto:example@email.com?subject=Website%20Inquiry"> Contact Support </a>

Adding CC and BCC Recipients

Include additional recipients using cc and bcc parameters:

<a href="mailto:primary@email.com?cc=copy@email.com&bcc=secret@email.com"> Send email with CC and BCC </a>

Pre-filling Email Body

Add pre-written content to the email body using the body parameter:

<a href="mailto:example@email.com?subject=Hello&body=Hi%20there%2C%0A%0AI%20wanted%20to%20reach%20out..."> Send pre-written email </a>

URL Encoding

Special characters in mailto links must be URL encoded:

  • Space = %20
  • Line break = %0A
  • Question mark = %3F
  • Ampersand = %26
  • Equal sign = %3D

Best Practices

  • Use clear link text - Make it obvious the link will open an email client
  • Consider fallbacks - Not all users have email clients configured
  • URL encode parameters - Ensure special characters work correctly
  • Keep it simple - Don't overload with too many parameters
  • Protect against spam - Consider obfuscating email addresses

Common Use Cases

  1. Contact forms and support links
  2. Feedback buttons
  3. Report issue links
  4. Newsletter subscriptions
  5. Business contact information
